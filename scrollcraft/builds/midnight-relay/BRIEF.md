# Internet Repair Crew

## Creative direction

The direction is a clean, modern interface with visibly broken components and the energy of a motorsport repair bay. The original cursor crew and four-beat structure carry forward.

| Topic | Direction |
| --- | --- |
| Vibe | Clean, industrial, fast, controlled. Graphite panels, hard edges, bright safety orange. |
| Journey | Boot the stalled archive, reconnect its navigation, rebuild a fragmented camera frame, read the restored garage log. |
| Energy | Tense idle, a precise cable snap, a rapid sequence of three photo locks, then a quiet finish. |
| Emotion | Intrigue, satisfaction, impact, resolve. The last piece locks in and the car’s lights hit all at once. |
| Signature move | The same three cursors physically trace, carry, and lock real page components into place. Scroll back and the repair comes apart. |
| Visual range | Industrial editorial with a strict grid, large condensed headlines, clean controls, and visible seams in broken components. |
| Structure | Live surface. One working archive stays in view as it changes state. The repair log doubles as chapter navigation. |
| Assets | One generated in-world photograph of a fictional rally car at a night garage. Cursor shapes, circuitry, and layout are vector or code. |

The audience is anyone who enjoys a short kinetic web experience. The last thing they can do is **Open frame**, which opens the full recovered photograph. The project’s own title is Internet Repair Crew. The world inside it is **Midnight Relay**.

## Storyboard and scroll score

| Beat | Before → during → after | Scroll range and device | Phone composition |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Boot | Archive stalled → the cursor crew comes out of standby → missing route flagged | 0–15%. Layered arrival and shallow depth. | Headline above a compact board. Three crew labels stay on the board. |
| Reconnect | Archive links idle → Patch traces the route and Fetch seats the connector → Frame and Log links become usable | 15–37%. Pinned wiring pass and state change. | The cable traces inside the board. Links stay in a single tab row. |
| Recover | Three scattered image pieces → Patch traces the frame, Fetch carries, Set seats each piece → the frame locks and warm color comes in | 37–80%. Three anchored assembly passes, then a reveal. This is the peak and gets the longest range. | The same board keeps the frame centered, with the fragments tucked into its corners. |
| Restore | Log line missing → a cursor carries it into place → the restored line and full photo open as real HTML controls | 80–100%. Note wipe and stationary hold. | The note sits immediately under the board. Controls remain reachable at large text sizes. |

The line at the end reads “First start. Six months in the garage. Worth every night.” The final few percent of scroll hold the finished frame and log so the visitor can take them in. With reduced motion or **Skip to repaired page**, the archive opens fully restored, with the photo and log available immediately.

The empty local fingerprint registry has no prior builds to compare against. This project uses an operable archive with a continuous cursor crew rather than a film, typographic poster, split comparison, gallery, chaptered article, worldflight, or rapid cut sequence.

## Camera frame

Built-in ImageGen produced the fictional still. The original was encoded as `public/midnight-relay.webp` for the site. Each tile uses the same photograph, so the pieces line up on the final frame.

Prompt:

> Generate a single wide 3:2 editorial photograph as an in-world recovered camera frame for the website Internet Repair Crew, a fictional midnight rally garage. Powerful modern automotive campaign photography, but real in-world and unbranded. A muscular silver-gray vintage 1980s boxy two-door gravel rally coupe, custom widened stance, front air dam and two sharp amber round auxiliary lamps, low front three-quarter camera angle, parked half out of a concrete industrial garage bay at night, engine just started. Car faces camera and points slightly toward right. Center the whole vehicle and keep silhouette of the entire car visible, with breathing room at top and edges for a responsive crop; headlights and wheel arches visually distinct. Tactile asphalt and brushed metal, fine suspension dust, a little cold white garage spill, intense amber from the car's pair of fog lamps, graphite shadows, subdued olive accents, warm lights reflected in the dark wet road. Strong diagonals, clean composition, believable documentary flash mixed with overhead practical light, high contrast without crushing car details. Crisp fine film grain, no fake UI overlay, no captions, no decorative typography, no identifiable logos, no readable license plates, no people. This photograph will be cut into three equal vertical pieces, so place readable material detail in each third and keep the centered focal subject across the inner thirds.
